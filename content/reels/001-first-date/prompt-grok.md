# SuperGrok Prompt — Reels 001

Attach `character/references/alice-master-face.png` as the mandatory visual identity reference.

Prepend `prompts/identity-lock.md`.

Create a photorealistic vertical 9:16 Instagram Reels video, 8–9 seconds long.

Alice is a 40-year-old blonde woman living in Saint Petersburg. She is getting ready for her first date after a long break.

SCENE:
Evening in a tasteful modern Saint Petersburg apartment. Soft cloudy city light comes through a large window. The atmosphere is intimate and realistic, not glossy advertising.

0–2 seconds:
Alice stands near a mirror and gently adjusts her hair. She looks focused and slightly nervous.

2–5 seconds:
She picks up a small handbag and earrings from a table. She briefly looks at her reflection. The emotion is subtle anticipation before an important meeting.

5–7 seconds:
She turns toward the camera. The camera makes a very slow, natural push-in. Alice looks directly into the lens.

7–9 seconds:
A restrained, confident smile appears. She then leaves the frame toward the apartment door.

WARDROBE:
Elegant black dress, mature and stylish, not overly revealing.

VISUAL STYLE:
Photorealistic contemporary smartphone footage with cinematic quality. Natural skin texture, realistic hair and fabric motion, realistic hands, soft evening light, shallow depth of field, restrained handheld camera movement.

IMPORTANT:
The woman must remain exactly the same Alice from the reference image throughout every frame. Identity consistency is the highest priority.

DO NOT ADD:
text, subtitles, logos, watermarks, other people, facial redesign, age reduction, different hairstyle identity, plastic skin, exaggerated model posing.

CORE EMOTION:
“I’m 40. I haven’t dated in a long time. Tonight I decided to try again.”
