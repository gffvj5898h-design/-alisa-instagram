# Reels 001 — Concept

## Status

Ready to generate in SuperGrok. Not generated in chat: video reference slot is required.

## Role in the series

Launch reel. Does not show the date. Creates a cliffhanger for the next posts.

## One-line

Алисе 40. После долгой паузы она впервые собирается на свидание.

## Why this cut works

- Hook in 1–2 seconds: woman at the mirror, getting ready, not posing.
- Watch-through: small nervousness → bag → look to camera → exit.
- Fits Alice: adult, calm, slightly ironic, not desperate.
- Serial: next pieces can be the road, «рассказывать, как прошло?», aftertaste, red flags.
- Identity risk is real: mirror + hands near hair. Keep motion small.

## What we do not show

- The man
- The restaurant
- The date itself
- On-video captions (add in Instagram)

## On-screen text in Instagram only

«Мне 40. Сегодня у меня первое свидание за очень долгое время.»

## Production choice

Preferred path:

1. Reference-to-video with `alice-master-face.jpg` as `<IMAGE_1>` / Character Reference.
2. If the first frame must match a still, approve a still first, then image-to-video with minimal motion.

Do not run text-to-video.
