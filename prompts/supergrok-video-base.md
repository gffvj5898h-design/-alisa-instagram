# SuperGrok Video Base Prompt

## Mandatory input

Attach: `character/references/alice-master-face.jpg`

Then prepend the full content of:

`prompts/identity-lock.md`

## Default technical specification

- vertical 9:16
- Instagram Reels
- photorealistic
- natural body and facial movement
- realistic hands
- natural skin texture
- restrained camera movement
- no subtitles unless explicitly required
- no logos or watermarks
- one continuous identity throughout the clip

## Rule

Never generate an Alice video from text alone. The master face image must be passed to SuperGrok as the visual reference for every Alice video.
